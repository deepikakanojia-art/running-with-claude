Question · Tokens

What are tokens in Claude?

✅ Correct! Tokens are units of text/data that models process. Example: A long document uses more tokens than a short paragraph — one reason it helps to give Claude only the relevant information it needs.

💭 Apply it to your work: Where could you reduce unnecessary information or provide only the most relevant content when working with Claude?

Question · Role + Goal + Tone

Why include a role, goal, and tone in a prompt?

✅ Correct! Role, goal, and tone make your instructions clearer. Example — Role: Act as an L&D instructional designer. Goal: Create 5 formative assessment questions. Tone: Simple and encouraging.

💭 Apply it to your work: Think of one prompt you regularly use. How could you add a role, goal, or tone to make it clearer?

Question · Evaluate and Iterate

Claude gives you an answer, but it isn't quite right. What should you do?

✅ Correct! Prompting is often an iterative process. Review, give feedback, and refine the output. Example: "The questions are too difficult for beginners. Simplify the language and make the incorrect options obviously wrong."

💭 Apply it to your work: What is one Claude output you've received recently that you could improve by giving it more specific feedback?

Question · Skeptical Prompting

What is the purpose of a skeptical prompt?

✅ Correct! A skeptical prompt helps you stress-test your thinking. Example: "Act as a skeptical reviewer. Look at this onboarding plan and identify the 5 biggest risks or gaps I may have missed."

💭 Apply it to your work: What idea, plan, or deliverable in your current work could benefit from Claude challenging it before you move forward?
